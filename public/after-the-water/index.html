Money you spend because of the loss is often part of the claim — tarps, a hotel, a dehumidifier, meals when you have no kitchen, a locksmith. Keep every receipt and log it here the same day. Whether any of it is covered depends on your policy; recording it costs nothing and not recording it cannot be undone.
