Before You File

Offline citation verification worksheet. Nothing you paste leaves this page.

Read this first. Being self-represented does not lower the standard. Courts have sanctioned pro se filers for citations that turned out not to exist — fees, dismissal with prejudice, and appellate findings of bad faith. A judge is entitled to assume every case you cite is real and says what you say it says.

This page cannot tell you whether a citation is real. Nothing offline can. It builds the list of things you must go and check yourself, and gives you somewhere to record that you did. It is not legal advice.
